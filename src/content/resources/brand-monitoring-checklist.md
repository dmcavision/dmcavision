A monitoring program needs clear boundaries and decisions, not only a list of search terms. Use this checklist when designing or reviewing coverage.

## Rights and reference data

- [ ] Current trademarks, logos, product imagery, and packaging
- [ ] Official accounts, domains, sellers, distributors, and campaigns
- [ ] Known licensees and territory restrictions
- [ ] Priority products, executives, and customer-support identities

## Detection coverage

- [ ] Marketplaces and social platforms
- [ ] Search, advertising, websites, domains, and app stores
- [ ] Relevant languages and regional variations
- [ ] Visual, textual, account, and destination signals

## Review workflow

- [ ] Confidence and harm-based priority rules
- [ ] Human validation standards
- [ ] Evidence capture fields
- [ ] Legal, security, fraud, and communications escalation

## Program measurement

- [ ] Validated incident rate
- [ ] Time from detection to review
- [ ] Outcome and relisting status
- [ ] Repeat actors and unresolved priority exposure

Review the checklist regularly as products, channels, threats, and platform policies change.
