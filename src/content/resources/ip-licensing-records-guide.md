Enforcement accuracy depends on knowing not only what an organization owns, but also what it has authorized. Fragmented licensing data creates false positives and slows review.

## Record the essential terms

Capture the licensor, licensee, protected assets, permitted media, territory, duration, exclusivity, sublicensing, modification rights, and termination status.

## Connect agreements to assets

Link the executed agreement to specific images, videos, marks, software versions, or catalogs. Broad folder names are rarely enough for operational review.

## Track authorized channels

Record approved domains, accounts, distributors, agencies, campaigns, and marketplace sellers. Note where authorization is conditional or region-specific.

## Manage changes over time

Preserve amendments, renewals, expiration, and termination notices. Reviewers need to know what was authorized on the date the online use occurred.

A structured license registry prevents unnecessary reports and gives rights holders a stronger basis for action when use falls outside the permission granted.
