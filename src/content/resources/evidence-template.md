Complete this checklist for each online location under review. Adapt the level of collection to the purpose, risk, and applicable requirements of the matter.

## Case identification

- [ ] Assign a unique case reference.
- [ ] Identify the rights owner and protected work or mark.
- [ ] State the purpose and scope of the collection.
- [ ] Record the collector or responsible reviewer.

## Source details

- [ ] Save the complete direct URL.
- [ ] Record the platform, domain, and page title.
- [ ] Capture stable post, listing, product, seller, or account IDs.
- [ ] Note whether login, region, language, or device settings affected access.

## Capture record

- [ ] Record date, time, and time zone.
- [ ] Capture the full relevant page context.
- [ ] Include visible account, seller, uploader, or domain information.
- [ ] Capture captions, descriptions, pricing, timestamps, and engagement details where relevant.
- [ ] Record video timestamps or interactive steps needed to locate the material.

## Files and integrity

- [ ] Preserve permitted downloads in their original form.
- [ ] Keep originals separate from cropped, resized, or annotated copies.
- [ ] Use consistent filenames and version labels.
- [ ] Record file hashes where appropriate to the purpose of the case.
- [ ] Store the evidence in an access-controlled location.

## Context and comparison

- [ ] Link a reliable reference copy of the original work or protected asset.
- [ ] Document the relevant similarities or use without altering the original evidence.
- [ ] Record facts that may indicate authorization, licensing, or an exception.
- [ ] Separate observations from conclusions requiring legal analysis.

## Case history

- [ ] Record every report submission and recipient.
- [ ] Save delivery confirmations and case numbers.
- [ ] Retain platform responses and requests for more information.
- [ ] Document changes, removals, relisting, or restoration.
- [ ] Record transfers or access when chain of custody is material.

Evidence requirements can vary substantially when litigation, regulatory action, or a contested claim is anticipated. Seek qualified guidance for those circumstances.
