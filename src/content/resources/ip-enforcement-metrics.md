Takedown volume is easy to count but difficult to interpret. A strong measurement framework connects activity to risk reduction and decision quality.

## Exposure metrics

Track validated incidents, affected assets, priority markets, audience or sales signals, consumer risk, and unresolved high-impact cases.

## Operational metrics

Measure time from detection to review, evidence completeness, duplicate rate, escalation time, and submissions per validated case. Speed should not come at the expense of accuracy.

## Outcome metrics

Record removal, restriction, rejection, clarification, restoration, and account-level action. Use consistent labels across platforms.

## Durability metrics

Monitor relisting, repeat actors, mirror domains, reappearance time, and whether the underlying network remains active.

## Quality metrics

Review false-positive findings, withdrawn reports, platform feedback, and decisions changed after specialist review.

Use metrics to improve priorities and controls, not to reward report volume. The most useful dashboard shows whether meaningful exposure is declining and why.
