Photographs are easily copied from portfolios, social platforms, marketplaces, and client websites. A clear rights workflow helps photographers distinguish authorized publication from misuse.

## Preserve creation records

Keep original files, embedded metadata, editing history, shoot dates, model or property releases, contracts, and publication records. Registration may provide additional benefits depending on jurisdiction.

## Document licenses precisely

Record the licensee, image, media, territory, duration, exclusivity, and permitted modifications. Unclear licensing records can make enforcement slower than detection.

## Monitor in context

Reverse image matching can find visually similar uses, but each result needs review. Portfolio features, news reporting, client use, and platform previews may have different authorization or legal context.

## Build a complete report

Connect the original image to the exact reported URL, account, screenshot, and capture time. State the requested action accurately and retain the response.

For disputed licenses, fair-use questions, or commercial claims, obtain appropriate legal guidance.
