Online content usually depends on several services. A page may be hosted by one provider, indexed by a search engine, promoted through social media, and monetized through a marketplace or payment service. Each layer can take a different action.

## Platform reporting

The platform controlling a post, profile, or listing may remove content or apply account-level restrictions under intellectual property or policy rules.

## Hosting-provider notices

A host can affect the source website when a supported complaint falls within its role and process. Identifying the actual provider may require infrastructure research.

## Search removal

Search de-indexing can reduce discovery but generally does not remove the source page. It should not be recorded internally as source removal.

## Select the route deliberately

Consider the right involved, responsible party, available evidence, urgency, and desired result. Avoid sending identical allegations indiscriminately to services with different roles.

Track each submission separately. Clear outcome labels help teams understand which layer acted and whether additional monitoring remains necessary.
