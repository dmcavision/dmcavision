Use this checklist to organize a potential DMCA takedown request before submission. Platform requirements vary, and this resource is general information rather than legal advice.

## Rights and authority

- [ ] Identify the original copyrighted work.
- [ ] Confirm the current rights owner.
- [ ] Record any registration, publication, assignment, or licensing information relevant to the work.
- [ ] Confirm that the person submitting the notice is authorized to act for the rights owner.
- [ ] Check whether the reported use may be licensed or otherwise authorized.

## Infringement locations

- [ ] Save each direct URL containing the allegedly infringing material.
- [ ] Record stable post, listing, account, channel, or product identifiers.
- [ ] Separate unrelated platforms or responsible parties into distinct case records.
- [ ] Verify that every URL is accessible and displays the material described.

## Evidence package

- [ ] Capture screenshots that include the material and surrounding page context.
- [ ] Record the capture date, time, and time zone.
- [ ] Preserve relevant account, seller, domain, or uploader details.
- [ ] Retain original files separately from annotated working copies.
- [ ] Link the reported material to a reference copy of the original work.

## Recipient and submission route

- [ ] Identify the platform, host, marketplace, or other service able to take the requested action.
- [ ] Use the service's designated form, rights-owner portal, or published agent details.
- [ ] Review the recipient's current evidence and formatting requirements.
- [ ] Understand whether the request affects the source content, a listing, or only search visibility.

## Notice review

- [ ] Clearly identify the protected work.
- [ ] Clearly identify the exact material and location being reported.
- [ ] Include current contact information.
- [ ] Include the required good-faith and accuracy statements.
- [ ] Include the appropriate physical or electronic signature.
- [ ] Remove unsupported claims and confirm that the requested action is specific.

## Submission and follow-up

- [ ] Save an exact copy of the final submission.
- [ ] Retain delivery confirmation and the platform case number.
- [ ] Record requests for additional information and all responses.
- [ ] Track whether the material is removed, restricted, rejected, restored, or relocated.
- [ ] Monitor for relisting and connect repeat activity to the original case.

For disputed ownership, fair-use questions, counter-notices, or high-risk matters, consider obtaining advice from qualified legal counsel before proceeding.
